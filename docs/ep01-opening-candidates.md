# EP01 Opening Candidates

These are proposals for a new revision, not changes to the accepted film or
claims about audience retention. Reference: accepted EP01 revision
`286fbb6553fde95a5e0d`, frames 000001, 000091 and 000151; approved style in
`approved-video-style.md`. Preserve the existing spatial orientation and label
illustrative feed motion as such.

1. **Shell to gears (selected).** The shell moves aside continuously and reveals
   the two feed gears and filament by about three seconds. Ask: “一根软线，怎么被稳稳推入喷嘴？”
   This directly connects a visible action to the episode's question and reuses
   existing geometry. Explain that filament must remain stiff where pushed.
2. **Follow the filament.** Move continuously from the drive gears down to the
   hotend. Ask how one filament can transmit force before softening. The cold
   and hot regions need clear visual evidence; the camera path and lighting
   require more work to keep the path understandable.
3. **Two gears, one filament.** Reveal the paired gears as the shell opens, then
   widen to locate the assembly in the toolhead. Ask how both sides cooperate
   to feed one filament. The explanation must avoid claiming simulated contact
   or manufacturing precision and preserve gear orientation.

Option 1 is selected for a future preview because the sequence is already
supported by the retained frames and follows the approved continuous style.
It is a creative hypothesis, not evidence that a new opening outperforms EP01.
Any new render, audio or edit requires its own review and must not overwrite
the accepted original.
